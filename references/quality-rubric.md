# Quality Rubric

Use this rubric before delivering drafts that must preserve structure and match or exceed source quality.

## Structure

- Same heading hierarchy or an explicitly approved equivalent.
- Same sequence of ideas and paragraph functions.
- No missing major claims, examples, objections, or conclusion moves.
- No accidental reordering that changes causality or emphasis.

## Meaning

- Factual claims, numbers, names, dates, definitions, and causal links remain accurate.
- The author's stance is preserved unless the user requested a new stance.
- Added context is clearly supported by the source or marked as an optional enhancement.

## Originality

- Sentences are rebuilt, not lightly synonym-swapped.
- Distinctive metaphors, jokes, uncommon turns of phrase, and highly recognizable lines are replaced.
- Paragraph rhythm and transitions are refreshed enough that the draft reads as a new piece.
- Paragraphs are generated from function and outline, not by following the original line by line.

## Similarity Risk

- No repeated use of the same paragraph openings as the source.
- No copied clause sequence, distinctive conclusion wording, or recognizable section landing.
- No unchanged rare metaphors, slogans, jokes, or signature aphorisms.
- Keyword clusters are natural for the topic, not suspiciously close to the source.
- Examples preserve function, but their narration is rebuilt.

## Readability

- Paragraphs have clear topic movement.
- Transitions feel natural.
- Tone fits the target audience and platform.
- No generic filler, empty slogans, or inflated phrasing.
- The draft has at least the same clarity, specificity, and emotional force as the source.

## Internal Final Checklist

Use this compact checklist internally before delivery. Do not include it in the final output unless the user explicitly asks to see the quality check, review notes, or process explanation.

```text
质量检查：
- 结构：保留原文层级与论证顺序
- 信息：关键事实、观点和因果关系未改写失真
- 原创表达：按段落功能重写，句式、衔接和比喻已重构
- 相似风险：已检查段首、金句、比喻、例子叙述和结尾落点
- 可读性：语言流畅，节奏和目标平台匹配
```

## Publication-Ready Delivery

After checks pass, deliver only the article itself:

- No "改写说明".
- No "质量检查".
- No "结构分析".
- No hidden-process explanation.
- No postscript about similarity risk or originality unless requested.
- If saving to a file, write only the publishable draft content to the file.

## Repair Gate

If any check fails, repair only the failed section first:

- Structure drift: restore heading order and paragraph function.
- Meaning drift: restore fact locks, logic locks, and stance locks.
- Similarity risk: change paragraph opening, clause order, rhetorical device, and section landing.
- Quality drop: strengthen specificity, transitions, examples, and rhythm.
